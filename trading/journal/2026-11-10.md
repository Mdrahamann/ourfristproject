## Rules (closed candles only)
- **Swing:** fractal with N=2 on 5m, 15m and 4H, and N=3 on 1H. A swing is usable only after bar i+N closes; that time is recorded as "knowable".
- **Sweep:** a single bar trades beyond a confirmed, untaken swing and closes back inside it. Multi-bar failed breakouts are not counted (a limitation).
- **BOS / CHoCH:** a close beyond the latest confirmed opposite swing. It's a CHoCH if it flips the prior break direction.
- **Displacement:** range ≥ 1.5 × ATR(14) and body ≥ 60% of range.
- **FVG:** 3-candle imbalance at least 0.1 × ATR, tracked until it's touched or fully filled.
- **Candidate:** sweep, then displacement and BOS/CHoCH within 16 bars, then an unfilled FVG. Anything short of that is Watch.
- **Conflict:** a later 15m break in the opposite direction downgrades a Candidate to Watch (conflict).
- **5m confirmation:** a 5m BOS/CHoCH in the trade direction after the zone is touched.

**Cost assumptions:**
- Taker fee 0.05% per side (VIP0, no BNB discount)
- Slippage 0.02% per side
- Spread from the CoinGecko snapshot

Cost in R = (0.14% + spread) × price ÷ risk. **No backtest has been run, so no win rate is reported.**
