## Why the rules produced almost no trades (diagnosis, development + test data)
Of **825 complete crypto 15m sequences** (sweep → strong candle → structure break → price gap) and 135 stock-linked ones:

| What stopped the trade | Crypto | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| Price reached TP1 before ever retesting the zone (E3, no chasing) | 366 (44%) | 58 |
| The price gap was fully filled before confirmation (my proposed cancel rule) | 201 (24%) | 16 |
| The confirmation close came after the worst acceptable fill (2R net no longer possible) | 98 (12%) | 14 |
| No untaken target available (T1) | 94 (11%) | 21 |
| No 5m confirmation within 60 minutes (C3) | 44 | 6 |
| Opposite 15m break, expired, or invalidated | 22 | 19 |
| **Entered** | **0** | **1** |

**What this shows:**
- **There's rarely room for 2R.** From the middle of the zone to TP2, the typical reward is only **about 1.1R**, and **just 13%** of setups even had 2.2R available. The stop beyond the sweep extreme, combined with targets at the nearest untaken high or low, rarely leaves room for 2R after costs.
- **Price usually doesn't wait.** TP1 typically sits only **about 0.55R** past the zone, so price reaches it before the retest.

## The $20–$30 per day goal under your risk rules
| Example | Result |
|---|---|
| 0.25% risk on a **$1,000** balance | $2.50 per trade. A 2R win ≈ $5 |
| Making $20 in one 2R win needs $10 risk | That needs a **≥ $4,000 balance** **and** a stop of at least about 1%, to stay within the $1,000 notional cap |

- With a smaller balance, **$20–$30 days aren't reachable without breaking your risk rules**, and size won't be increased.
- So expect the "$20–$30 days" share to be **0% at small balances**. That's the honest outcome, not a failure of tracking.
